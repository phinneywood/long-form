Privacy

Long Form stores your sign-in email, Send-to-Kindle address, feeds, delivery settings, and delivery metadata to provide the service.

Article content is fetched to build EPUB files. Prepared emails and EPUB attachments are stored privately for reliable retries, then removed on the daily cleanup after a completed or failed send is seven days old. Article URLs, titles, delivery status, and provider references remain in delivery history; this is not a permanent article archive. Authentication codes and sessions expire.

Long Form uses Supabase for application data, Resend for email delivery, Vercel for the web interface, and Amazon Send to Kindle as the destination you configure.

If you connect Long Form to ChatGPT or another MCP client, Long Form uses OAuth to authorize that client to access the permissions you approve. Tool requests may include article URLs, source URLs, editor settings, or requests to read delivery history. Long Form returns only data associated with the connected Long Form account. The client provider may process tool inputs and results under its own privacy terms.

You can stop future MCP access by disconnecting Long Form in the client. OAuth access and refresh tokens expire and may be revoked.

This is a beta policy and will be expanded before broader release.
