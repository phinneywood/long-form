Privacy

Long Form stores your sign-in email, optional Send-to-Kindle address, subscribed sources, editorial preferences, delivery settings and delivery metadata to provide the service.

Extracted original articles and editions are retained privately for the reader and library, along with reading position, saved material and editor conversation history. Prepared emails and EPUB attachments are stored privately for exact sends and reliable retries. Completed or failed send payloads are removed on the daily cleanup after they are seven days old. Article URLs, titles, delivery status and provider references remain in delivery history. Authentication codes and sessions expire.

Long Form uses Supabase for application data, Resend for email delivery, Vercel for the web interface, OpenAI for editorial composition and discussion, and Amazon Send to Kindle as an optional destination you configure.

To compose editions or answer your questions, Long Form sends OpenAI relevant article extracts, your editorial guidance, and source, reading or delivery context. These requests use the API with response storage disabled. Your original reading and chronological feed remain available independently of the AI editor.

If you connect Long Form to ChatGPT or another MCP client, Long Form uses OAuth to authorize that client to access the permissions you approve. Tool requests may include article URLs, source URLs, editor settings, reading discussion or requests to read delivery history. Long Form returns only data associated with the connected Long Form account. The client provider may process tool inputs and results under its own privacy terms.

You can stop future MCP access by disconnecting Long Form in the client. OAuth access and refresh tokens expire and may be revoked.

This is a beta policy and will be expanded before broader release.
